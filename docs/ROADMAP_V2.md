# ProDental Roadmap V2

## Current Phase

Production readiness and product consistency for the non-PMS platform. The live product scope is maintained in `CURRENT_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`.

## Near-Term Sequence

1. Production environment, security, backup, queue, scheduler, mail, and storage gates.
2. Release-baseline review and deployment rehearsal.
3. Verification and Clinic operational refinement.
4. Reporting and product analytics improvements.
5. Approved public onboarding and invitation workflow.

## Future Integration Boundaries

These are not part of the current Organization Operations Workspace:

- PMS integrations
- Claims workflow
- Clinical records
- Treatment planning

Appointments and Calendar are now core Clinic modules and are not part of the deferred PMS scope.

## Governance

Future modules must reuse AppShell, PDS, Work Context Engine, policies, tenant scope, and public IDs.
